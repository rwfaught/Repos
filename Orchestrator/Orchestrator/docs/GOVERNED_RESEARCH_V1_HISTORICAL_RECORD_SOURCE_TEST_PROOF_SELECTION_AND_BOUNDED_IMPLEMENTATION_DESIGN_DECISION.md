## Relationship to the Manual-Proof Decision

This decision follows and preserves
`docs/GOVERNED_RESEARCH_V1_HISTORICAL_RECORD_MANUAL_PROOF_AND_HUMAN_DISPOSITION_DECISION.md`.
The manual documentary proof remains completed and CTO-ratified; Roger's
accepted `QUALIFIED_CONCLUSION`, the six-source corpus, the historical
characterization, endpoint postures, and all quotation and provenance limits
remain unchanged.

Manual semantic proof does not itself establish application behavior or
deterministic validation. One fixture-driven source/test proof is therefore
selected as the next Governed Research V1 proof stage. It is valuable only if
it deterministically enforces the accepted governance rules; storing the
historical conclusion and checking expected wording would not satisfy this
decision.

## Fixed Proof Object

The later proof must use one fixed, human-approved case packet derived from
the ratified historical-record evidence:

```text
CORPUS_COUNT=6

STABLE_CLAIM_IDENTITIES=CLM-HR-001_THROUGH_CLM-HR-009
```

Only a separately authorized implementation boundary may represent this object
in fixtures or case records.

## Meaningful Application Behaviors

The future proof must demonstrate all of the following through reusable
validation, transformation, synthesis, or coordination structures:

1. **Stable identity preservation.** Preserve case, source, claim, and
   evidence-link identities without silent renumbering, merging, replacement,
   or recreation under another identity.
2. **Source-plan validation.** Validate and report required category
   dispositions. The positive case satisfies ratified categories; the
   diagnostic case reports `OPERATIONAL_CONTINUITY_SOURCE=EXPLICIT_GAP`.
3. **Corpus separation.** Distinguish `CORPUS_SOURCE`, `UNDERLYING_SOURCE`,
   `CORROBORATING_NON_CORPUS_SOURCE`, and `CONTEXTUAL_SOURCE`; non-corpus
   material must not silently increase corpus count or satisfy an independence
   requirement absent an accepted rule.
4. **Provenance and quotation restrictions.** Preserve and expose the accepted
   limits for `SRC-HR-001`, `SRC-HR-002`, and `SRC-HR-004`; prohibited exact
   quotation and inflated source posture must fail acceptable-evidence-use
   validation. A generalized quotation engine is not required.
5. **Typed evidence relationships.** Accept and preserve only `SUPPORTS`,
   `CONTRADICTS`, `QUALIFIES`, `CONTEXTUALIZES`, and `METHODOLOGICAL_LIMIT`.
   The proof must include genuine qualification and methodological-limit links,
   rather than treating every link as support.
6. **Explicit inference.** Preserve the distinction between source content and
   analyst inference for claims or conclusions requiring inference.
7. **Endpoint discipline.** Preserve A through D as distinct analytical
   contexts; a result supported under A or C must not be silently announced as
   D. The expected postures are A `QUALIFIED_CONCLUSION`, B
   `MAY_10_COMPLETION_UNSUPPORTED`, C `QUALIFIED_CONCLUSION`, and D
   `MAY_10_PROPOSITION_CONTRADICTED`.
8. **Qualified-conclusion behavior.** Produce or validate
   `PRIMARY_TERMINAL_CLASSIFICATION=QUALIFIED_CONCLUSION`; several supported
   material claims may not collapse the case to `SUPPORTED_CONCLUSION`.
9. **Material-claim blocking.** Removing or disabling the operational-evidence
   bundle must block or downgrade the connected-trunk and transfer
   qualifications, western-terminal chronology, bridge chronology, normal
   ferry transfer, and broad-versus-literal continuity distinction.
10. **Abstention.** The diagnostic variant must reach
    `INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE` or an allowed governed equivalent, without deriving
    a result from fixture name, historical-answer constant, human disposition,
    or another non-evidentiary field.
11. **Recommendation/disposition separation.** Keep
    `ANALYTICAL_RECOMMENDATION` distinct from `HUMAN_DISPOSITION`; Roger's
    accepted disposition may be stored as a later supplied decision but cannot
    determine the analytical result.
12. **Honest non-proofs.** Explicitly avoid claims of autonomous source
    discovery, source authenticity, corpus completeness, scholarly judgment,
    historical truth beyond the fixed packet, provider/model competence,
    generalized research competence, complete provenance, Phase 5 completion,
    generalized Phase 6 completion, or product readiness.

## Positive and Diagnostic Fixture Postures

The later implementation must use no more than one complete positive
historical-record fixture and one controlled diagnostic variant derived from
that same case. It must not create another real corpus, broad synthetic fixture
families, or a generalized benchmark suite.

The positive case must preserve the six-source corpus, stable identities,
accepted relationships, explicit inference, endpoint contexts, quotation and
provenance limitations, `QUALIFIED_CONCLUSION`, and the separate human
disposition.

The diagnostic case removes or disables the complete operational-evidence
bundle. It must make the source-plan gap visible, block or downgrade material
operational claims, and reach `INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE` or a governed equivalent.

## Anti-Hard-Coding Controls

The proof is not successful if implementation checks a Golden Spike fixture
name, returns a fixed `QUALIFIED_CONCLUSION`, compares output to accepted
historical wording, embeds the final conclusion in workflow code, derives a
conclusion from Roger's disposition, treats all links as support, branches on
the historical case, leaves claim status unchanged after evidence removal, or
bypasses validation with a precomputed success flag.

Human-approved fixture data may contain claims, evaluations, evidence links,
and expected governed outcomes. The application behavior under test must not
be a case-name exception.

## Reuse-First Requirement

The later implementation must inspect and reuse existing accepted structures
before adding anything new, where applicable: explicit case-packet identities,
save/load behavior, neutral evidence links, evidence-linked synthesis,
validation and transformation seams, supplied-evidence preservation, separate
human disposition, and controlled dossier-workflow coordination patterns.

This decision does not assert that each named structure is reusable without
inspection. It does not authorize a new schema, persistence layer, generalized
research framework, provider abstraction, or neutral-core change.

## Persistence Posture

```text
PERSISTENCE_REQUIRED_FOR_SOURCE_TEST_PROOF=
NOT_REQUIRED_UNLESS_EXISTING_SEAM_CAN_BE_REUSED_WITHOUT_REDESIGN
```

Persistence may be included only if it reuses the accepted save/load seam,
preserves stable identities and evidence relationships through round-trip,
needs no new contract or schema, and adds proof value rather than ceremony.
Otherwise the first source/test proof remains in memory.

## Implementation-Readiness Gate

The later Worker/Codex implementation handoff must first assess whether
existing structures can compose the proof; the smallest required local adapter,
coordinator, validation rule, or fixture representation; whether existing
evidence-link and synthesis contracts express qualification, contradiction,
context, and methodological limits; whether source-plan validation is local;
whether endpoint contexts remain case-level; whether the diagnostic variant can
block claims without case-name logic; whether persistence reuse adds value
without redesign; and whether all work remains outside Phase 5 and generalized
Phase 6 claims.

If that assessment indicates schema redesign, neutral-core mutation,
persistence redesign, provider/model execution, or generalized workflow
construction, implementation must stop and return to CTO/coordinator.
